# Put Pocketbook on your home screen

The app needs to be on a website once so your phone can install it. GitHub Pages does this for free.

1. Sign in at github.com and create a new **public** repository called `pocketbook`.
2. Click **Add file > Upload files**, drag in everything from this folder, and click **Commit changes**.
3. Go to **Settings > Pages**. Under Branch pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
4. Wait a minute. Your app is at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/pocketbook/`.

**iPhone:** open that link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
**Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

It opens full screen like a normal app and works offline.

## Good to know
- Data is saved on your phone only. Use **Settings > Download backup** now and then.
- To move data from the Claude version: Download backup there, then **Restore backup** here.
- AI sorting only works in the Claude version. Keyword sorting, learning, and your rules all work here.
- The repo is public but your data isn't in it. It lives only in your phone's browser storage.
